Sunsets at Mughal Gardens & Hidden Tombs

Escape the city noise and discover 5 golden-hour Mughal ruins tucked inside Delhi's quietest green pockets.

1. Isa Khan's Sunken Octagonal Tomb

Pre-dates Humayun's Tomb with exquisite blue tiles and sunken gardens.

2. Jamali Kamali Mosque Ruins

Tucked deep inside Mehrauli Archaeological Park featuring red sandstone Mughal poetry carvings.

3. Safdarjung Tomb Gardens

The last grand garden tomb of the Mughal Empire, perfect for sunset silhouettes.
