Midnight Paranthas & Secret Spices of Chandni Chowk

Picture this: it's past 11 pm, smoke curls up from a dozen tawas at once, and a parantha stuffed with rabri is sizzling in pure ghee. Welcome to Purani Dilli.

"This isn't just a food street. It's a 350-year-old living museum where every dish comes with a royal story."

The Iconic Legendary Eats

1. Paranthe Wali Gali

Frying deep-stuffed paranthas since 1870. Don't miss the Rabri Parantha and Papad Parantha.

2. Karim's (Near Jama Masjid)

Mughlai kebabs and slow-cooked mutton nihari made with royal recipes passed down since 1913.

3. Old Famous Jalebi Wala

Thick, syrup-soaked, crispy golden jalebis fried fresh in pure desi ghee at the corner of Dariba Kalan.
