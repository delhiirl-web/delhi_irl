Essential Traveler's Blueprint

Plan Your Visit to Delhi

From golden winter mornings and the world-class metro grid to budget projections, here is your essential guide to experiencing Delhi effortlessly.

Best Time to Visit

Delhi experiences significant seasonal shifts. Choose your travel window to match your outdoor and weather comfort:

Oct – Mar (Best) Crisp winters, blooming gardens, perfect for outdoor walks (10°C – 24°C).
Feb – Mar (Spring) Amrit Udyan opens, pleasant breezy evenings and colorful flower festivals.
Jul – Sep (Monsoon) Lush green parks and hot street chai, with humid afternoon spells.
Apr – Jun (Summer) Intense heat (up to 44°C). Best for dusk walks, AC museums, and evening bistros.

How to Reach

Indira Gandhi International (IGI)

Terminals T1 & T3 handle global and domestic carriers. The Airport Express Metro connects directly to Central Delhi in just 19 minutes.

Major Railway Terminals

New Delhi (NDLS), Old Delhi (DLI), Hazrat Nizamuddin (NZM), and Anand Vihar (ANVT) link all major Indian express and Vande Bharat routes.

Inter-State Bus Hubs (ISBT)

Kashmere Gate, Anand Vihar, and Sarai Kale Khan connect interstate Volvo and AC bus fleets smoothly to neighboring states.

Getting Around

Delhi Metro (DMRC) ↗

Clean, air-conditioned, world-class network spanning 12 lines. Grab a rechargeable Smart Card or a 1-to-3 day Tourist Pass for unlimited hops.

Cabs & Auto Rickshaws

Uber, Ola, and BluSmart (100% all-electric fleet) offer guaranteed door-to-door transit. Autos operate by digital meters or agreed rates.

Electric DTC City Buses ↗

Modern low-floor green and blue electric buses run frequently across inner rings, offering ultra-affordable hops with digital ticketing.

Estimated Daily Budget

Backpacker / Budget ₹1,000 – ₹2,000 / day

Metro connectivity, legendary street food feasts in Purani Dilli, lively hostel stays, and free monument garden walks.

Mid-Range Traveler ₹3,500 – ₹6,500 / day

BluSmart & Uber transit, boutique BnBs in South Delhi, upscale casual bistros in Khan Market, and guided museum entry.

Luxury Explorer ₹12,000+ / day

5-star heritage hotels (The Imperial, ITC Maurya, The Lodhi), fine dining at Bukhara or Indian Accent, and private chauffeured luxury transit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Delhi IRL and who creates this content?
Delhi IRL is an independent city discovery collective and digital publication documenting Delhi's heritage lanes, street culinary spots, local markets, and cultural scenes without generic tourist checklists.
What is the most reliable way to navigate Delhi as a visitor?
The Delhi Metro (DMRC) is the fastest, cleanest, and most economical transit option covering major hubs across the capital. You can purchase a 1-day or 3-day unlimited Tourist Card or use all-electric BluSmart/Uber cabs for point-to-point travel.
Are the food spots and pricing on Delhi IRL verified?
Yes, all restaurants, street food hubs, and cafes featured across our guides are reviewed in person with verified operating timings, metro connectivity routes, and approximate price ranges for two people.
